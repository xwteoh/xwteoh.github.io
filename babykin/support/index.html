Common questions

How does a helper or grandparent join?

A parent opens Family, taps Invite, and picks the person's role and language. Babykin shows a QR code. The other person installs Babykin on their own phone, scans the code and types their name. They don't need an account.

Which languages does Babykin use?

English, Chinese, Bahasa Indonesia and Burmese. Each person sees Babykin in their own language.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Everything is saved on the phone first, and shared when the phone is back online.

Can we delete our information?

Yes. A parent can delete the household in the app. Privacy says what that removes.

Does Babykin give medical advice?

No. Babykin is a log. If you're worried about your baby's health, speak to a doctor.
