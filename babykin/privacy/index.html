Privacy

Draft. Babykin is still being made and isn't in the App Store or Google Play yet. This page describes how Babykin will handle your information in the closed beta. It will be checked again before then.

Last updated 4 November 2026

Babykin is a baby log that a household shares: parents, the helper, a confinement nanny and grandparents. This page says what Babykin keeps, where it's kept, and who can see it.

Babykin is made in Singapore by an independent developer.

What Babykin keeps

  • The log. Feeds, pumping, diapers, sleep, medicine, temperature, weight, baths, tummy time and notes, with the time of each one and who logged it. A note can include a photo.
  • The baby. Name and date of birth.
  • The household. Its name and time zone, and the house instructions that parents write, with their translations.
  • Members. Each person's display name, role and language.
  • Parents' sign-in. When you sign in with Apple or Google, they give Babykin an account ID and an email address. Apple lets you hide your real email address.
  • Helpers, nannies and grandparents don't make an account. Their phone signs in without one when they join from an invite.
  • Usage counts. How often each part of the app is used. These counts never include what you logged.

Babykin doesn't collect your location or contacts. It shows no ads, doesn't track you across other apps, and never sells your information.

Where it's kept

  • On each phone in the household, so Babykin works without a signal.
  • On Supabase's servers in Singapore, so every phone in the household stays up to date.
  • In a nightly backup on GitHub, in the United States. The backup is made on a GitHub server and encrypted before it's stored, with a key that only the developer holds.
  • House instructions are translated by Microsoft's translation service in Asia Pacific, so their text is sent there to be translated. Nothing else from the log is sent.
  • Alerts reach your phone through Expo, Apple and Google, so the words of each alert pass through them.

Who can see it

  • Only members of your household can see its log.
  • Only parents can invite or remove members, or change the house instructions.
  • When someone is removed, their access ends. The entries they logged stay in the log, with their name.
  • The developer can reach the stored information to keep Babykin running and to help when you ask. It isn't looked at for any other reason.

Deleting your information

  • Parents can delete the household in the app. That removes its log, members and photos from Babykin's servers. You can save everything as a spreadsheet file first.
  • Helpers, nannies and grandparents can ask a parent to remove them, or write to Babykin.
  • How long the encrypted backups are kept will be set before the beta.

Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act, you can ask what Babykin holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. The email address to write to will be added here soon.

Health

Babykin is a log. It doesn't give medical advice. If you're worried about your baby's health, speak to a doctor.

Changes

When this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will also be shown in the app.
