Join the family log

Someone in your family has invited you to Babykin, a shared log of the baby's feeds, diapers and sleep. You don't need an account.

  1. Install Babykin on your phone. It's coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.
  2. Open the invite again: scan the QR code, or tap the link.
  3. Type your name. Then you can start logging.

The invite works once, for 48 hours. If it has run out, ask for a new one.
